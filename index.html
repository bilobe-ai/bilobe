Our products

Purpose-built tools for clinical practice.

Your expert guide through medical knowledge

A conversational decision-support system grounded in medical guidelines. It works across clinical scenarios, from triage support for general practitioners to treatment adherence monitoring on hospital wards.

  • Europe firstHosted on European servers, with three sovereignty tiers: international cloud, European cloud or fully on-premise.
  • Guideline-grounded, human-validatedKnowledge comes only from guidelines and procedures curated and approved by our medical team.
  • Not just a RAGKnowledge graphs built on established medical ontologies guide retrieval through real relationships between clinical entities, not text similarity alone.

Helps you discover and define structure in your clinical notes

An AI agent system that drafts an eCRF from the data. It turns a clinician's high-level description into a structured variable registry, grounded in what the clinical notes actually contain.

  • For the human, through the humanIt starts from the clinician's description and a short conversation, then checks back at every step so variables can be edited or added.
  • Data-based, not eminence-basedFields come from real-world data, not abstract theory. Ambiguity stays with the decision maker, not with the person entering the data.

Making biomedical data ready for secondary use and sharing

A highly customisable pseudonymization system built for biomedical data. It runs entirely on-premise: no data ever leaves your institution.

  • Your data, your serviceRuns fully on-premise. Nothing is transmitted outside your infrastructure.
  • Plug and playDrag files into the interface for immediate pseudonymization. One-off use needs no setup.
  • Pipeline-ready middle layerConnect it to your existing data streams as transparent middleware that runs in the background.

Illustrative example with synthetic data. Try it: every element responds.
