Privacy & cookies.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. This page explains the little personal data that is still involved when you visit it or write to us.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Bilobe SRL, Via Ferrata 5, 27100 Pavia (PV), Italy. VAT and tax code 03039860188.

For anything about your personal data, write to info@bilobe.eu or, by certified email, to bilobe@namirialpec.it.

What this website collects

Nothing on purpose. The site has no forms, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no social media plug-ins. We do not build profiles of visitors and we cannot tell who has visited.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. To deliver a page, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers: your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser and operating system. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a website that works and is protected against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States; transfers of data rely on the safeguards described in GitHub's privacy statement, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

When you write to us

If you email us, we use your address, your name and what you write to answer you and to follow up on your request. The legal basis is the steps you ask us to take before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or our legitimate interest in answering (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We keep these emails for as long as the conversation and any resulting relationship require, then delete them, unless the law obliges us to keep them longer (for example for accounting). Our email providers process them on our behalf.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies. It saves one item in your browser's local storage, which is never sent to us or to anyone else:

NamePurposeTypeDuration
bilobe-langRemembers whether you chose English or Italian.TechnicalUntil you clear your browser data

Technical storage of this kind does not need your consent, which is why the site shows no cookie banner. If we ever add analytics, we will update this page and ask for your consent before anything that requires it is used.

Links to other websites

Links to LinkedIn, to our partners and to other websites open pages that we do not control. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 21 of the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to info@bilobe.eu; we answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority of the EU country where you live or work.

Changes to this page

We update this page when the website or the way we handle data changes. The date of the latest version is shown beside the text.

Nulla di proposito. Il sito non ha moduli, account, strumenti di analisi, pubblicità o plug-in di social network. Non costruiamo profili dei visitatori e non sappiamo chi ha visitato il sito.

Quando ci scrivi
